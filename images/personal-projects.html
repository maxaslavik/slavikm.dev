Project Aquila - Advanced Liquid Flight Vehicle and Regenerative Cooling

Chief Engineer and Propulsion Lead

Leading a dedicated team of 60 students to build Altair, a 1600 lbf liquid regen engine additively manufactured out of Al10SiMg alloy, and its flight vehicle Albatross, a 300lb liquid Nitrous Oxide / Alcohol rocket expected to launch in May 2027.

Helped create a work breakdown structure that places student experience first by assigning a component responsibility to each member, giving them the opportunity to bring their part from conception to flight and learn the entire design process.

Wrote a coupled combustion/CEA, multiphase flow, and regenerative cooling solver from the ground up in Python to optimize flight vehicle design.

Personally designed the Altair additive thrust chamber and taught regenerative cooling, pintle injector optimization, and finite element analysis to students on the Propulsion team.

RXπ — RPI Experimental Propulsion Initiative

RPU-1 "Reliant": RPI's First Liquid Rocket

Chief Engineer

Led the engine and feed system design for a brand-new team building RPI's first liquid rocket engine, with no institutional playbook to build from. Most of what the team knows now was earned the hard way over the course of design and fabrication.

Wrote a custom Python implementation of the Non-Homogeneous Non-Equilibrium (NHNE) model to predict multiphase nitrous oxide flow through the injector, and a MATLAB 2D heat-transfer solver to propagate inner wall temperature over burn time.

Attempted static fire in Spring 2026. Had to abort due to a malfunctioning solenoid valve on the GN2 pressurization line. The team is running tests to identify cause of valve failure and get RPU-1 back to the test site.
RPI Design/Build/Fly — 2024–25

Load-Bearing Pylon Design for Competition Aircraft

Subsystems Lead

As subsystems lead, I designed and optimized a set of load-bearing pylons to hold maximum payload while staying aerodynamic, with quick attach/release to the wing structure per the competition's scoring rules.

Used FEA, carbon fiber sheet composite, and deliberate 3D-print layer orientation to put strength exactly where it mattered. I also designed an embedded quick-release pin system.

RPI DBF finished 5th place worldwide, out of 100+ teams. For the mission focused on the load bearing pylons, RPI scored second place.
